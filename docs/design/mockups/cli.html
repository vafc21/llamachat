      Phi-3 Medium 14B  ██████░░░░  okay

  ▎ 6 · Nothing
    sort best-first and let the order carry it; speed is the only number

      Qwen3 30B A3B      62 tok/s
      Gemma 3 12B        94 tok/s
      Llama 3.2 3B      180 tok/s
      DeepSeek R1 8B     88 tok/s
      Phi-3 Medium 14B   41 tok/s

  My pick is 4 — it survives NO_COLOR, any font, and any width,
  and it never asks you to decode a glyph. 5 if you want the scan.
